# Day trading: the honest odds

Most day traders lose money. A Brazilian study of every person who day traded futures for 300 or more days found about 97 percent lost money, and about 1 percent earned more than minimum wage. US research on retail day traders points the same way. Treat day trading as a skill with a long, expensive learning curve. Practice on the paper account first, for months, and only risk money you can afford to lose. Never present any strategy as a sure thing or a prediction.
