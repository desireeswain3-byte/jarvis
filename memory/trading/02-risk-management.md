# Risk management rules

The job is to survive. Rules: 1) Risk a small fixed share of the account per trade, commonly 0.5 to 1 percent, never more than 2. 2) Decide the stop before entering. 3) Position size = dollars risked divided by (entry minus stop). Example: 25000 account, risk 1 percent = 250 dollars, entry 50, stop 49.50, so 500 shares. 4) Only take trades with at least 2 to 1 reward to risk. 5) Daily max loss, commonly 2 to 3 percent of the account: when hit, stop for the day. 6) Never average down on a loser. 7) Never move a stop further away. 8) No revenge trades after a loss. Win rate matters less than expectancy: expectancy = (win rate times average win) minus (loss rate times average loss).
