# Order types and execution

Market order: fills now at the best price, can slip on fast or thin stocks. Limit order: fills at your price or better, may not fill. Stop order: becomes a market order when price touches the stop. Stop limit: becomes a limit order, may skip your exit in a fast move. Bracket order: entry plus a profit target and a stop together, the best habit for beginners. Spread: the gap between bid and ask is a cost on every trade. Slippage: the gap between expected and actual fill. Avoid trading the first minutes after the open and illiquid stocks unless you have a plan for the wider spreads.
