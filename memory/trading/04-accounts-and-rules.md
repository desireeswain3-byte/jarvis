# Accounts and rules

The US pattern day trader rule is gone. The SEC approved FINRA's change on April 15, 2026. It removed the four-day-trades-in-five-days test and the 25000 dollar minimum, and replaced them with an intraday margin framework. It took effect June 4, 2026, and firms that need time have until October 20, 2027, so each broker may still apply the old limits for a while. Always check what the specific broker does today. Cash accounts avoid margin entirely but must wait for funds to settle (US stocks settle the next business day). Fees, spreads and data costs reduce returns. Profits are taxed, and wash sale rules can disallow losses on repeated trades in the same stock. Ask a tax professional.
