# Reading the tape: volume, VWAP, levels

Relative volume: today's volume compared with the normal for that time of day. Above 1.5 to 2 times normal means real interest. VWAP: the volume weighted average price of the day. Price above VWAP tends to mean buyers are in control, below means sellers. Many traders only take longs above VWAP and shorts below. Support and resistance: prices where the stock reversed before, such as yesterday's high and low, premarket high and low, and round numbers. Moving averages like the 9 and 20 period EMA show short term trend. Gaps: the stock opens away from yesterday's close, usually on news.
