# Common day trading setups

Opening range breakout: mark the high and low of the first 5 to 15 minutes, trade a break with volume, stop on the other side or at the midpoint. Gap and go: a stock gapping up on news with strong relative volume, buy the first pullback that holds support. VWAP reclaim: a stock sells off, crosses back above VWAP on rising volume, stop just under VWAP. Pullback in a trend: a rising stock pauses near the 9 or 20 EMA, buy the bounce with a stop under the pullback low. Failed breakout fade: a breakout that quickly falls back inside the range. For every setup write down: the trigger, the stop, the target, and the reason it should work. If a setup has no written rule, do not trade it.
