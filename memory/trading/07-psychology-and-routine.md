# Trading psychology and routine

Common mistakes: oversizing after a win, revenge trading after a loss, trading out of boredom, chasing extended moves, holding losers and cutting winners, ignoring the stop. Routine: before the open, check news, gaps and levels and write a plan of at most three stocks. During the day, take only planned setups and stop at the daily loss limit. After the close, journal every trade with the reason, result in R (multiples of the amount risked) and a lesson. Review the journal weekly. Sleep, food and breaks affect decisions. Never trade while upset, rushed or tired.
