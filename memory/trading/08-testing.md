# Backtesting and paper trading

Test an idea on past data and on the paper account before risking money. At least 100 trades per setup are needed before the numbers mean much. Beware overfitting, where a rule only fits the past, and survivorship bias. Count fees and slippage. Paper results flatter you because fills are perfect and emotions are absent, so move to small real size only after consistent paper results over months, and then scale slowly.
